##  Project Overview
This proposal outlines a comprehensive digital marketing strategy for Woodiva Home, a brand specialized in modern furniture and smart space-saving solutions. The project aims to transform Woodiva’s digital presence into a high-converting sales engine through data-driven strategies and creative storytelling.

---

## Project Objectives
- Establish Woodiva as a pioneer in functional modern furniture.  
- Build a seamless customer journey across digital platforms.  
- Generate qualified leads through targeted campaigns.  
- Strengthen brand positioning in the local furniture market.  

---

##  Project Scope
- Content marketing focused on Smart Living storytelling.  
- Social media optimization (Instagram & Facebook).  
- Meta Ads strategy for conversions.  
- Competitor benchmarking and market analysis.  
- Audience segmentation (Homeowners & Interior Designers).  

>  **Final Deliverable:** A fully integrated digital marketing strategy and professional brand portfolio ready for market launch.
